Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Kappi Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own TikTok account.

Data the app uses

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues after the owner authorizes the app. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to call the TikTok API on the owner's behalf.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's account (display name, open ID) to confirm which account is connected.
  • The videos, captions and publishing settings the owner chooses to upload.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from any other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app settings; the locally stored tokens are then deleted.
